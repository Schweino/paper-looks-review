Paper looks review

0 of 25 decided

Thank you for helping. We're making a printed family newspaper that's mailed to a grandparent each month. Families add photos and short notes, and the paper is laid out for them in the look they choose. Here are all 25 looks. Each shows the same two made-up months: 3 posts (2 pages) and 6 posts (3 or 4 pages). The name and address at the top left is where the grandparent's address prints for mailing.

Tap any page to see it large. For each look, pick Keep, Change or Drop, and add a note if something looks off or you love it. Your picks are saved in this browser, so you can come back later. When you're done, press Copy my notes and send the result to whoever sent you this link, by text or email.

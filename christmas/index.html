45 sleeps until Christmas

Her Christmas paper, in the mail before the 25th

The family's December photos in a Christmas look, four pages in big, clear type, mailed First Class to arrive before Christmas. Then a new paper every month.

Start by Friday, December 4. Add photos until Sunday, December 6. It arrives by about December 19.

Start by Friday, November 20 and her November paper comes too, arriving about December 3: two papers by Christmas.

Start a paper See the 20 Christmas looks

$8.99 a month for her paper, December's too, printing and postage included. No paper that month? No charge.

Want only the Christmas paper? It goes to the printer December 9 and is mailed within a few days. Cancel after it is mailed, and nothing more is charged.

Cancel before your first paper goes to the printer and we refund you in full, less the PayPal fee on a yearly plan. Skip the coming month from your paper's page or its preview, or cancel online any time.

Giving it as a gift? You pay and run it, or give it to someone else to run.

How December works

  • December 6The last day to add the family's photos and notes for the December paper.
  • December 7We email you the paper to look over, and you can add a short letter for page 2.
  • December 9It goes to the printer and is mailed within a few days, First Class, to arrive by about December 19.

It is the same paper as every month: the same price, the same four pages, and the same ways to skip a month or cancel. Only December's dates and look are different. $8.99 a month for your paper. $3.99 a month for each other house we mail it to.
